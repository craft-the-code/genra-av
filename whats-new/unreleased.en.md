Genra responds faster, tells you what it is doing, and catches more kinds of malware.

- Scans show a progress bar; "Your computer" opens faster and says what it is checking instead of staying blank.
- A button that is working shows its own spinner; the rest of Genra stays usable.
- When a new version is out, Genra shows what is new; update now or later.
- Genra catches more kinds of malware: inside archives, scripts, shortcuts, programs disguised as documents, older Office files.
- Common, confirmed malware is quarantined by itself; programs with a valid digital signature always ask you first.
- USB worm: Genra moves the fake shortcuts away and shows the hidden folders and files again; your data is untouched and it can be undone.
